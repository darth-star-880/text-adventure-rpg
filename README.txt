A feature-packed, text-based RPG built entirely in a single Python script as a personal coding challenge!


Key Features

 -Dynamic Shop System: Buy and sell raw materials, items, books, and liquids with real-time stock tracking and custom price calculations.

 -The Thief Encounter: Watch out! A sketchy area event might cause a thief to rob your wallet (or get caught red-handed).

 -Armor Lottery: Spend your gold for a chance to win rare armor pieces with special discounts.

 -Stat Randomization & Progression: Equip weapons and armor that interact with a custom stat randomizer, affecting your hero's health, stamina, mana, strength, dexterity, and defense.

 -Crafting System: Combine specific items, books, and potions in your inventory to forge custom-named legendary gear with stat boosts.

 -Turn-Based Combat: Face off against enemies like Goblins and Dragons in a tactical combat loop with defense scaling and damage controls.

 -Easter Eggs: Random book quotes from Harry Potter and other fictional tomes, plus a few surprise browser triggers based on your net worth!

Requirements
 -Python 3.x installed on your system.

 -No external libraries required (uses built-in modules like random, time, webbrowser, subprocess, and math).
